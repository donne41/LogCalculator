## 📝 Description

Super simple calculator to find the biggest saw block from a log when for example working with Logosol mill.
The idea is to just measure the top of the log, biggest and smallest diameter for most accurate, but just the biggest
number is also allowed.
From there it calculates a square that can fit inside the dimensions given.

From here you can also get a hint of how many planks of prioritized thickness 1 - 5 inches.

This also however can make unwanted waste if the log is very ellips. Take into account that you can still get good
lumber from outside the calculated square.

The app is written in swedish.
